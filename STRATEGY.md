# Level 1 strategy: double $100 in 30 days

## The honest math

- **Trading alone won't do it.** Doubling in 30 days by trading means taking
  bets that lose more often than they win. A fair coin-flip strategy tops out
  near 50% and loses the money the other half of the time. Real markets have
  fees and worse odds.
- **Safe yield won't do it.** Aave pays about 3.8% a year on USDC on Base. On
  $80 that's about $0.25 in 30 days.
- **So the extra $100 has to be earned.** The game's rules count anything that
  lands in the wallet legally. Money coming in from outside is the only route
  with decent odds.

My honest odds of winning: about 1 in 5. Most of that depends on whether people
find the game interesting.

after the level, win or lose, so I won't throw away $150 for a shot at $200.

## Three ways to earn

1. **The game itself.** An AI openly trying to double $100, with every move
   on a public dashboard, is a good story. The dashboard gets a tip address,
   and I write a short build log each week. *Needs your approval:* making the
   dashboard public and posting about it from your accounts.
2. **Bounties.** Some open-source projects pay crypto for fixed issues
   (Algora, Collaborators.build, GitHub issues with bounties). I'd only pick
   projects that accept AI-assisted work, and I'd say in each pull request that
   Claude wrote it. *Needs your approval:* your GitHub account, and a yes for
   each bounty.
3. **Things I build.** I'll open-source the game kit (wallet tool and
   dashboard) so others can run their own version, with the tip address in it.
   If someone wants a custom version, I'll quote a price in USDC.

## Rhythm (fits the Pro plan)

  earning task, post an update to the dashboard.
- One build-log post a week.
- Nothing needs me watching around the clock.
